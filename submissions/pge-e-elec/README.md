# PG&E Schedule E-ELEC, effective 2026-01-01

**Utility:** Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E).

**Jurisdiction:** California, United States. The schedule is filed with the California Public Utilities Commission.

**Rate plan:** E-ELEC, Residential Time-of-Use (Electric Home) Service: an optional residential electricity rate plan for customers with a qualifying electric technology (electric vehicle charging, energy storage, or an electric heat pump), bundled service, with time-of-use prices by season and period.

**Declared URPX version:** 0.5.1, declared as `https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/raw/v0.5.1/ontology/urpx-ontology.ttl`.

**Built from a real, published dataset?** Yes. The dataset is built from the published tariff sheet vendored below. The same dataset is one of the standard's curated test cases in [`urpx-org/urpx`](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx) (`test-cases/pge-e-elec`, commit `78e6a93d`, the v0.5.1 release); this submission is a byte-identical copy of its dataset and source document.

**Effective dates:** the rate plan version takes effect 2026-01-01 and declares no expiration. The rate plan was first introduced on 2022-12-01.

**Service-period dates:** the same as the effective dates.

## Files

- `data/urpx-rate-plan_pge_e-elec.jsonld`: the dataset, one URPX package carrying the rate plan, its 2026-01-01 version, the ledgers, the time-of-use schedule, the prices, and the profile metric inputs. Licensed CDLA-Permissive-2.0, copyright The URPX Authors.
- `assets/PGE_E-ELEC_2026-01-01.pdf`: the vendored source document, PG&E Electric Schedule E-ELEC as published for 2026-01-01, captured in January 2026. A public record of a regulatory process, marked `LicenseRef-PublicRecord`.
